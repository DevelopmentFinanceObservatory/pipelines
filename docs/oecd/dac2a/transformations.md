## Download the source data

The OECD publishes DAC2A in April, June, September and December. The complete table is
downloaded from the OECD each time it is republished, and replaces the previous copy,
so the data reflects the OECD's latest release.

## Standardise providers and recipients

Provider and recipient codes are standardised to Data Commons IDs used across the
platform, so data can be compared across datasets. The totals DAC2A reports for groups
of providers, such as all DAC countries, and for groups of recipients, such as regions
and income groups, are kept as providers and recipients of their own. Where two provider
codes name the same organisation and report the same figures, the figures are kept
once.

## Select figures

Only figures in current US dollars are included, not constant US dollars. Only flows to
recipients on Part I of the DAC List, the developing countries eligible for ODA, are
included. Flows to Part II recipients, the countries in transition, were official aid
rather than ODA, though DAC2A reports them under the same types of aid.

## Convert to whole units

DAC2A reports amounts in millions, which are converted to whole units. ODA per person
and ODA as a share of GNI are kept as reported.

## Select dimensions

Every figure is reported against the year, the provider and the recipient. DAC2A is a
table of disbursements, so every flow is recorded as a disbursement, and its type of aid
says which line of the table it is, such as grants, repayments, interest received, or
debt forgiven or rescheduled. Each flow is counted gross or net as DAC1 counts lines of
the same kind. Repayments, interest received, recoveries and the offsetting entry for debt
relief are net. Grants and rescheduled debt are gross. A flow is marked as neither where
DAC2A does not say which. DAC2A reports total ODA, and ODA loans and other
long-term capital, gross under one type of aid and net under another. Each pair is
published under a single type of aid, with the gross and the net figure told apart by
their netting basis alone. Flows are split into grants and non-grants where the type of
aid is wholly one or the other. Imputed multilateral ODA, the OECD's estimate of each
provider's share of multilateral organisations' ODA, is marked as modelled. Net ODA as a
share of the recipient's GNI records that it is a share of GNI, and net ODA per person
that it is divided by the recipient's population.
