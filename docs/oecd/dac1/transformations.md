## Download the source data

The OECD publishes DAC1 in April, June, September and December. The complete table is
downloaded from the OECD each time it is republished, and replaces the previous copy,
so the data reflects the OECD's latest release.

## Standardise providers

Provider codes are standardised to Data Commons IDs used across the platform, so data
can be compared across datasets. The totals DAC1 reports for groups of providers, such
as all DAC countries, the G7 and the EU members of the DAC, are kept as providers of
their own.

## Select figures

Only figures in current US dollars are included, not constant US dollars or providers'
own currencies. Amounts received are left out, though net disbursements, which subtract
them, are kept. Where DAC1 reports a grant equivalent under two types of aid, the fuller
of the two is used, and total flows as a share of GNI in grant equivalents are left out,
since they repeat ODA as a share of GNI.

## Convert to whole units

DAC1 reports amounts, GNI and population in millions, which are converted to whole
units.

## Select dimensions

Every figure is reported against the year and the provider. Flows are broken down by
type of aid as DAC1 reports it, and by gross or net disbursements, commitments or grant
equivalents, split into grants and non-grants where DAC1 splits them. Shares record that
they are a share of the provider's GNI. GNI and population are reported about the
provider.
