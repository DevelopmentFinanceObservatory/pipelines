## What this dataset holds

The bilateral climate finance each party reports in Table III.1 of its Biennial Transparency Report to
the UNFCCC, by party, year and status. A committed total is published as a commitment and a disbursed
total as a gross disbursement. Each figure sums the party's types of funding (ODA, other official flows
and other) at face value. The US dollars are each party's own-currency amounts converted at the OECD
DAC exchange rates.

Each figure is the party's climate total over all recipients, so its series name ends "to all
recipients" and it carries no split by objective. Rows that report only a grant equivalent, or no
status, are left out.

Each party works out its climate amounts with its own method, by fixed coefficients or by assessing
each project, so every figure carries `measurementMethod` `Estimated`.
